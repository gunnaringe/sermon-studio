Privacy

What the hosted studio at my.sermon-studio.app stores, where, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. If you run Sermon Studio yourself, or use the desktop app, everything stays on your own server or computer, and none of this reaches us.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no tracking, analytics or adverts. It loads nothing from other sites. Your browser remembers the language you choose here, and nothing else. The website is served by Cloudflare, which sees your IP address in order to deliver the pages.

What the studio stores

Your login

You log in with Pocket ID, a login service that runs on the same server as the studio, so your login is not handed to anyone else. The studio keeps your ID there, and the name and email address you gave there. The name and email are only shown to the admins of your churches; they are never used to give access. While you are logged in, your browser has a session cookie, and the studio keeps a hash of it and the token from the login, so that it can log you out of Pocket ID too.

Churches and members

For each church: who its members are and whether each is an admin. Invite links are kept only as a hash, with their expiry. Each church has its name, logo, default tags, processing, and intro and outro clips.

Recordings

The recordings you upload, and the files made from them. With each recording: the edit (sections, cuts, labels, tags, processing), when it arrived, how each file was made, and when the latest change was made. A waveform and a listening preview are kept in a cache, to make the editor fast.

Shares

A share is a code that lets someone without a login send recordings to a church, or download a sermon. The studio keeps a hash of the code, its settings, and for each upload or download: the file name, its size, the IP address and the time. The church's members see this list.

Logs

The studio logs what it does: recordings received, files made, renamed and deleted, and changes to churches and members. For the public share page, it logs the IP address of uploads, downloads and wrong codes, to stop abuse. It does not log which pages you look at.

In your browser

The session cookie while you are logged in (30 days), and a short-lived cookie while you log out, and one with the church you last opened (a year), so the start page can take you back to it. Local storage keeps your language, theme and a few view settings, the church you last used, and the undo steps of the last 20 recordings you edited. Nothing in your browser is used for tracking.

Where it is stored

On a server in the EU, rented from Contabo and run by us. The recordings are not used for anything but making the church's files.

Who processes it

Each church decides what it uploads and who its members are. Sermon Studio stores and processes it on the church's behalf. It is not sold, and not used for anything else.

Processed for us by: Cloudflare (delivering this website and the studio's traffic) and Contabo (the server, in the EU). The studio and the login (Pocket ID) on it we run ourselves.

How long it is kept

  • Recordings and files: until the church deletes them. A deleted recording stays in the church's trash for 30 days, then it is removed for good. The original can also be deleted on its own, once the files are made.
  • The cache: removed after about 30 days without use.
  • Shares: until they are removed. An ended share is removed after 30 days, unless the church chooses 7, 90 or 365 days, or never. Its list of uploads and downloads goes with it.
  • Sessions: 30 days, or until you log out.
  • Your login: until you ask us to delete it. Leaving a church, or being removed from it, ends the membership at once.
  • Logs: kept for a limited time.

Having it deleted

A church's admins can delete recordings (on the recording's page, then Settings → Trash → Delete now to remove it at once), remove members and remove shares. A member can leave a church in Settings → Members.

To have your login, or all of a church's data, deleted, or to see what is stored about you, write to contact@sermon-studio.app.

About Sermon Studio

I made Sermon Studio for my own church. Every Sunday the service is recorded, and someone has to cut out the sermon, even out the sound and publish it, often late in the evening in a busy week. I wanted a way to do that which is easy and quick: open the recording, mark the sermon, press Save.

It is free and open source. If it can help your church too, you are welcome to use it.

Questions, or just want to say hi? Write to contact@sermon-studio.app.
