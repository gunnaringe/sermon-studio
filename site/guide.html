Using Sermon Studio

A short tour, in the order you meet it. The pictures show a real speech: President Kennedy's inaugural address (1961), uploaded as a WAV.

The recordings page

All the church's recordings, by year. to do means not cut yet; done means saved, with files. The search finds names, dates and labels. Click a row to play the sermon, download it or add labels.

Uploading

Drop a file at the top of the page, or choose one. The date is taken from the file name or the file; change it if needed, and give the recording a name if a day has two. The upload goes in parts, so it goes on after a dropped connection. It then opens in the editor.

Marking the sermon

The editor shows the whole service as a waveform. Silences are grey. A new recording starts with the sermon set to all of it but the silence at its start and end.

Sections are what you keep: drag their edges, or set the start and end at the playhead. Drag on an empty part of the waveform to add another section; what is between sections is left out. Cuts take a part out of a section, such as a cough or a song. Each can have a label. Hear join plays a join as it will sound.

Your changes are kept as you go, also if you close the page. Undo and Redo are in the bar at the bottom.

Processing

How the sermon is made into files: mono or stereo, noise reduction, how much of the room's sound to keep, leveling, compression and the loudness. Hear a sample makes 30 seconds to listen to first. A recording starts with the church's defaults, set in Settings → Processing.

Intro and outro

Upload the church's clips in Settings → Intro and outro, and choose the default intro and outro. In the editor, each sermon can use the default, another clip, none, or a clip of its own. The clips are brought to the sermon's loudness, with a short pause at each join. With an intro or outro, the sermon on its own is made too.

Saving and the files

Choose the formats under Output: FLAC, MP3 and Opus. Save keeps the edit, then makes the files that are missing or out of date. The work goes on if you close the browser. A file made from an older edit says out of date on the recordings page.

Downloading

On the recordings page, a sermon's Download button lists its files, with their sizes: with the intro and outro, or the sermon only. A format not made yet can be made from there.

Sharing

Share for download… on a finished sermon makes a code and a link. Someone without a login can then download it. You choose the files, and if you want, how many downloads and an end date.

The other way works too: under Shares (the icon in the header), make a code that lets someone send recordings to the church without a login, such as the sound technician.

More

About Sermon Studio

I made Sermon Studio for my own church. Every Sunday the service is recorded, and someone has to cut out the sermon, even out the sound and publish it, often late in the evening in a busy week. I wanted a way to do that which is easy and quick: open the recording, mark the sermon, press Save.

It is free and open source. If it can help your church too, you are welcome to use it.

Questions, or just want to say hi? Write to contact@sermon-studio.app.
